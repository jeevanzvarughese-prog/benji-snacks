ORDER & PICKUP POLICY

Please read before ordering

If you don't pick up your order:
Orders not picked up within 48 hours are considered forfeited. Card payments are not refunded for uncollected orders.

If we can't fulfill your order:
If an item goes out of stock after ordering, you'll be offered a substitute of equal value or a refund.

Payment disputes:
Card disputes go through Stripe's own process. Cash/Venmo issues are resolved directly with the seller.

Not covered:
Items lost or damaged after pickup, allergic reactions, or delays caused by school schedule changes.
